The Atelier

The art of restraint

Maison Éternelle began with a single idea: a wardrobe built on black and white can dress any moment, from a morning meeting to a midnight dinner. We cut fewer pieces, and we cut them better.

Our patterns, pinstripe, houndstooth, gingham and herringbone, are drawn from the great tailoring traditions, then refined until nothing is left to remove.
